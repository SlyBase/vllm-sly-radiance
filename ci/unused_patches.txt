patch_async_dynwidth.py   dynamic verify width under async scheduling; depends on patch_dynwidth. A/B 2026-09-23 (0.3.1, prod args, 300 W, ABAB, conc 2/4/8/16 x 48): RADIANCE_DYNAMIC_WIDTH=1 costs 3 % at conc 4-16 (C8 401/406 -> 389/397), conc 2 unchanged -- rejected
patch_dflash_calib.py     drafter activation-statistics collection for quantize_dflash_mxfp4.py (offline tooling)
patch_dflash_selector_topk.py  A/B 2026-09-22 (BetterBench, prod args, 300 W): K 24/32 vs 16 = +0.8/+0.9 % weighted serial decode but -2.9/-2.5 % conc 1 (conc-1 noise 0.1 %), acceptance unchanged; K != 16 also needs its own torch.compile cache (the candidate_selector graph is cached at the checkpoint's 16 and replays with a shape assert)
patch_dflash_mxfp4_kv.py  DFlash2 fused KV for an MXFP4-quantized drafter; our drafter is W4A16 (sly/patch_dflash_w4_packed.py)
patch_draft_attn_blockm.py  rejected upstream (4-34% slower on gfx1201), not wired in there either
patch_dynwidth.py         scheduler-side dynamic verify width; A/B 2026-09-23 rejected, see patch_async_dynwidth.py
patch_gdn_glue.py         strided GDN gates + empty core_attn_out; only meaningful on the R4D GDN path, which bf16 SSM state declines here
patch_gdn_merge_inproj.py GDN in_proj single-GEMM merge (radiance_gdnmerge.py not copied). Upstream's merged forward replaces forward_hip with the AITER GDN core; this image serves forward_cuda (FLA). A forward_cuda-sliced variant was A/B'd 2026-09-23 (0.3.1, prod args, 300 W): step gap 35.31 -> 35.15 ms, prefill -1.5..-2.2 %, conc 4 -2.5 %, KV pool 384,316 -> 375,820 (the torch.cat copies fragment the weight arena) -- rejected. A K != default merge also needs RADIANCE_GDN_MERGE_INPROJ in envs.compile_factors (stale AOT graph otherwise)
