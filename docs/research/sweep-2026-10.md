# Corpus sweep, 2026-10-25 — what the LLM-research corpus has for this image

Corpus [`1jmdev/research`](https://github.com/1jmdev/research) at `ae20e11f`, 73
subcategories, top 3 per subcategory read (219 papers, TL;DR depth). Every number
below carries a label: **measured** (here or in the repo's own docs), **calculated**
(arithmetic on our data, not run on the GPU), **paper-claim** (from the digest only),
**guess** (not measured, not derived).

**Verdict: the corpus does not contain a large lever for this image.** 2 of 219 papers
are "take", 13 are "partly", 204 are rejected. No paper offers more than ~10 % without
training, multi-GPU or a new model. The one lever with a real size is not in the corpus
at all: `SamplingParams.thinking_token_budget` already ships in vLLM 0.32 (code fact,
`sampling_params.py:424`), so a thinking budget needs a launch config, not a feature.

The frame for every speed claim: decode runs at 34–36 ms/step against a bandwidth floor
of 32.5 ms (calculated: 18.3 GB per step ÷ 563 GB/s measured). Kernel work has at most
5–8 % left. The levers that matter are therefore **tokens per step** (acceptance) and
**tokens per answer** (thinking length), not the step itself.

## Own-code facts found on the way

- `SamplingParams.thinking_token_budget` exists in vLLM 0.32 (`sampling_params.py:424`),
  and `ReasoningConfig.reasoning_end_str` forces the reasoning end when the budget is
  spent (`config/reasoning.py:27`).
- `vllm/v1/worker/gpu/sample/trace_replay.py` ships in the pinned tree — offline replay
  of acceptance rules is possible without a GPU window.
- Prefix hit rate 56 → 77 % with `KVCACHE_EXACT` (measured); KV pool 436k tokens on fp8,
  943k with the int4 pins (measured).
- 300 W gives ~20 % more prefill and almost no decode gain (measured). The 210 W cap
  stays unless the owner orders otherwise.

## Open questions

1. How much quality does the acceptance rule actually cost at `temperature > 0`? No test
   exists — GSM8K is greedy and does not touch the rule.
2. Do the loops come from the acceptance lock-in at high `q`, or from the uniform drift
   (~40 nats per 1k output tokens, calculated)? The trace does not separate the two.
3. Is the +3.9 % Cactus gain typical? It comes from a single pathological prompt.

## Relation to `NOT-ADOPTED.md`

A rejected item goes to `docs/NOT-ADOPTED.md` when it was **measured or read in depth**
and failed a gate. This file only records the sweep-level triage: digest-level, no
full text, no run. A rejection here is a reason not to read further, not a gate result.
